# Interactive widgets

Interactive widgets let you embed live, manipulable figures directly in your pages.
Readers can drag a slider or toggle a series and watch the figure respond, with no JavaScript on your part.

Widgets work anywhere a figure does, including inside grids.
They fall back to a static image when scripting is unavailable.
